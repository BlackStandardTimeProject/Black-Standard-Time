About the project

Our time,our standard

Black Standard Time is a wall calendar that gives every day of the year a moment from Black history, paired with an original work of art.

Most of what we're taught about Black history is told through struggle. This calendar tells it through what we built, made, won and celebrated: the first headline, the album that changed the sound, the founding of a school, the night someone walked into a room nobody had walked into before.

Each date is researched and sourced. Each month is illustrated. The result is a year you can hang on the wall and live with, one day at a time.
