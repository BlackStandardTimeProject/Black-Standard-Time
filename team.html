The team

Who madethe year

Curator & creative director

Donye Taylor

Donye Taylor is the curator and creative director of Black Standard Time, and the founder of Nuclei and Mentally I'm Here. She shaped the vision for the calendar and chose every date, building the year around what Black people have created, won and celebrated.

@donyetaylor ↗

Collaborator · Artist

Fritz von Eric

Fritz von Eric is a Texas-born artist and content creator based in New York City. His lively, eye-catching figures explore Black life, family, queer identity and human connection with a warmth and playful energy that make even serious subjects feel close. He grew up seeing few reflections of Black people in the art world, so he makes the images he wanted to see.

@fritzvoneric ↗

Graphic designer

Maya

Maya is a UK-based graphic designer who shaped the look of the calendar, from its type and grid to how each month's art and dates sit together on the page.
